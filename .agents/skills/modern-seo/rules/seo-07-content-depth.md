# SEO Rule: Content Depth

Google categorizes content by depth. Match depth to intent.

## The Rule

Avoid **"Thin Content"**. Every page must offer unique value.

## Guidelines by Type

### Category Pages

- **Goal**: Moderate Depth.
- **Requirement**: Unique description above/below products.
- **Bad**: Just a grid of products (looks like duplicate content of other sites).
- **Good**: "We tested 50+ laptops. Here are the top 10 sorted by price..."

### Product Pages

- **Goal**: High Specificity.
- **Requirement**: Specs, real-world usage, comparison to similar models.

### Blog/Guide

- **Goal**: In-Depth (Skyscraper).
- **Length**: 1500+ words usually perform best for "Ultimate Guides".

## Checklist

- [ ] Does this page exist elsewhere on the web?
- [ ] Is there at least 300 words of unique text?
