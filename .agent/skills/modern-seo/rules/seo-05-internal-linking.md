# SEO Rule: Internal Linking

Internal links spread "Link Juice" (Authority) and help crawlers discover pages.

## The Rule

Every page should link to **3-5 related pages** within the content (not just nav/footer).

## Best Practices

- **Anchor Text**: Use descriptive keywords, not "Click Here".
  - ❌ "Check out this laptop"
  - ✅ "Check out the [best gaming laptops under $1000]"
- **Relevance**: Link to products/categoris naturally mentioned in the text.
- **Orphans**: Ensure no page is "orphaned" (unlinked).

## Checklist

- [ ] At least 3 links to other internal pages?
- [ ] Anchor text contains keywords?
